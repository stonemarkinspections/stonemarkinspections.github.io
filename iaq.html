What Indoor Air Quality Screening Helps With

IAQ screening is useful when buyers, sellers, or homeowners want more clarity about indoor conditions. It can help document concerns and guide smart next steps.

  • Musty odors or suspected moisture-related issues
  • Recurring condensation, humidity concerns, or ventilation questions
  • Allergy / sensitivity concerns for occupants
  • Basements, crawl spaces, and other moisture-prone areas
  • Homes that have been vacant or recently renovated
IAQ screening is designed to inform decisions. It is not a medical evaluation and does not diagnose health conditions.

What’s Included

The goal is straightforward: identify and document conditions that commonly impact indoor air quality and provide practical guidance on what to do next.

  • On-site review of common contributors (moisture, ventilation, visible conditions)
  • Targeted screening based on the home and client concerns
  • Clear documentation and reporting
  • Recommendations for next steps when concerns are identified

Why Professional Screening Beats Guesswork

Indoor air complaints are often treated with quick fixes — candles, filters, dehumidifiers — without understanding the root cause. A professional screening focuses on the building conditions that actually drive air quality: moisture control, airflow, and the spaces most likely to hide problems.

If more advanced testing is appropriate, you’ll be told exactly what to pursue and why — with documentation to support your decision.
