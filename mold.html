When Mold Screening Makes Sense

Many homes have areas where moisture has been present at some point: basements, crawl spaces, bathrooms, laundry rooms, and around HVAC systems. Screening is useful when buyers or homeowners want better clarity than “it smells musty” or “there’s discoloration over there.”

  • Musty odor, damp smells, or recent water intrusion
  • Visible staining or suspected microbial growth
  • Basements and crawl spaces with humidity concerns
  • Older homes or homes that have been vacant
  • Before closing on a purchase when moisture history is unclear

What This Service Includes

Mold screening focuses on building conditions and documentation — not guesswork.

  • Inspection of moisture-prone areas and visible conditions
  • Identification of common moisture sources (leaks, drainage, ventilation, condensation)
  • Photo documentation of suspect areas
  • Practical guidance on next steps and risk reduction
Screening is not a medical evaluation and does not diagnose health conditions. If lab analysis is needed, you’ll be advised on the appropriate next step.

Why “Mold” Is Often Misunderstood

Discoloration can come from many sources — including dirt, staining, or past moisture events. The real risk is persistent moisture and poor airflow. A professional screening helps identify whether the underlying conditions are present and whether further evaluation is warranted.
