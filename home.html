What a Home Inspection Covers

A standard home inspection is a visual evaluation of the major systems and components of the home, performed in accordance with recognized industry standards.

  • Roofing, flashing, and visible roof structures
  • Exterior siding, trim, grading, and drainage
  • Foundation, crawl space, and structural components
  • Electrical systems, panels, and accessible wiring
  • Plumbing systems, fixtures, and water heating equipment
  • HVAC systems and basic operation
  • Interior rooms, doors, windows, and finishes
  • Attics, insulation, and ventilation
