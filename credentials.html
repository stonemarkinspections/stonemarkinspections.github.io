Credentials & Certifications

StoneMark Inspections is built on formal training, recognized certifications, and ongoing education. The credentials below support the services offered and help ensure inspections are performed using current standards and best practices.

Professional Home Inspection

StoneMark Inspections is a member in good standing with :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0} (International Association of Certified Home Inspectors). InterNACHI membership requires adherence to a published Standards of Practice, a Code of Ethics, and ongoing continuing education.

Radon Measurement

Radon testing is performed by a certified Radon Measurement Professional through the :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1} (National Radon Proficiency Program). NRPP certification verifies formal training, examination, and compliance with national radon measurement standards for real estate transactions.

Pool & Spa Inspection

Pool and spa inspections are supported by specialized training focused on residential pool and spa systems, safety features, circulation equipment, and common installation or maintenance deficiencies. Evaluations are visual and non-invasive, consistent with industry inspection standards.

Mold & Moisture Assessment

Mold and moisture-related services are supported by training through the :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2} (ECAN). This training emphasizes building science, moisture dynamics, and appropriate assessment methods used to identify conditions that may support microbial growth.

Certifications and training support inspection services but do not guarantee the absence of defects. Inspections are limited to visible and accessible conditions at the time of inspection and are not technically exhaustive.
