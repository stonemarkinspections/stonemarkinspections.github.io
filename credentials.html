Radon Measurement

Radon testing is provided by an NRPP-certified Radon Measurement Professional. Certification supports formal training, exam-based qualification, and proper test documentation for real estate timelines.

Pool & Spa Inspection

Pool and spa inspections are supported by specialized training focused on residential pool/spa systems, circulation equipment, safety features, and common deficiency patterns. Evaluations are visual and non-invasive, limited to accessible components at the time of inspection.

Mold & Moisture Assessment

Mold and moisture screening is supported by training as a Certified Mold Assessor Technician through the Environmental Consultants & Affiliates Network (ECAN). This training emphasizes building conditions, moisture dynamics, and appropriate documentation methods for microbial growth concerns.

Certifications and training support inspection services but do not guarantee the absence of defects. Inspections are limited to visible and accessible conditions at the time of inspection and are not technically exhaustive.
